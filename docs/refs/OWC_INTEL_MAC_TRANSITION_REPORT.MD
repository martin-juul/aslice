## Captured material

The article reports macOS 27 as Apple-silicon-only and describes how owners can identify affected Intel Macs. It motivates maintaining software for machines excluded from new major OS releases. This secondary report does not establish an exact security-update end date or prove compatibility of a particular old Mac with aslice. The project platform window and hardware acceptance tests are independent requirements.

## Decision-relevant context

Source locator: OWC Wayne G, June 15, 2026; affected Intel models and how to identify your Mac.

The article frames macOS 27 Golden Gate as the first Apple-silicon-only major release. It directs users to About This Mac to distinguish an Intel processor from an Apple chip and discusses continuing with the last compatible OS as distinct from buying replacement hardware. Exclusion from a new major version is not immediate failure of the machine or all installed applications.

Because OWC is a secondary publisher, its outlook for continued use does not bind Apple to a particular security-update duration. Model-specific compatibility should be established from Apple's model record, such as the separate late-2013 Mac Pro entry.
